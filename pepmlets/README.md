# Medical Educational Pamphlets & Photos (पेम्प्लेट्स)

This directory is designated for clinical and patient educational pamphlets, drug monographs, dosage charts, algorithm flowsheets, and clinical photos in Nepal.

### Categories supported:
- **Patient Counseling Pamphlets (नेपाली तथा अंग्रेजी परामर्श पर्चा):**
  - Diabetes Care & Insulin Administration Guide
  - Hypertension & Low Sodium Lifestyle Brochure
  - Asthma Inhaler & Spacer Technique Leaflet
  - ORS & Dehydration Child Protocol (जीवनजल)
  - Antimicrobial Stewardship & Complete Course Compliance
  - Dengue Warning Signs & Fluid Therapy Home Monitoring
  - Tuberculosis DOTS Schedule & Nutrition
- **Clinical Algorithm Flowsheets:**
  - Sepsis 1-Hour Bundle
  - ACLS Resuscitation Flowchart
  - DKA Fluid & Potassium Titration
  - Acute Anaphylaxis Emergency Step-by-Step
